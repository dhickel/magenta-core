# Services Specification

## Intended Contract

Services own use-case behavior and hide persistence, transport, filesystem, and model-provider details from callers. `WorkspaceDirectoryService` owns descriptor resolution; explorer, metadata, action logging, execution, tool, prompt, and output services consume `WorkspaceDescriptor` without reconstructing identity. Add service behavior only for concrete user-facing workflows.
